# Instagram Launch Playbook: Local Trail Riders

Use this before pushing hard registration. The goal is to create real rider conversations first,
then invite the right people into LocoXperts.

## Positioning

Core promise:

> Find a local trail to ride this weekend, with route confidence and local support when you need it.

Do not lead with "register on our platform." Lead with a useful rider action:

> DM `RIDE` with your city and skill level. We will suggest a weekend trail idea.

## Instagram Bio

Short version:

> Local trail rides in Nepal  
> Routes, guides, bike prep, and rider support  
> DM `RIDE` for a weekend trail suggestion  
> Early access: [link]

More casual version:

> Want to ride but not sure where to go?  
> Nepal trails, local guides, and weekend ride ideas  
> DM `RIDE` with your city  
> [link]

## Link Setup

Use one link in bio. For now, point it to the most useful low-friction page:

- Best: a simple landing page or waitlist page.
- Acceptable now: homepage with a clear trail-rider CTA.

Recommended CTA text on the linked page:

> Tell us your city and ride level. We will help you find a local trail to ride.

Do not make account registration the first visible ask. Registration can come after the rider sees
value.

## DM Keywords

Start with only three keywords:

- `RIDE`: rider wants trail suggestions.
- `GUIDE`: rider wants local support or does not want to ride alone.
- `KORA`: rider wants preparation help.

Keep it manual at first. Manual replies teach you what people actually want.

## DM Reply Scripts

When someone sends `RIDE`:

```text
Nice. Which city are you riding from, and what kind of ride do you want this weekend?

1. Easy / beginner
2. Moderate trail ride
3. Long endurance ride
4. Not sure yet
```

After they answer:

```text
Got it. I can suggest a route idea based on that.

Are you riding solo or with friends?
```

When sending the platform link:

```text
This is exactly why I am building LocoXperts: to help riders find trails, local support, and ride plans around Nepal.

You can check the platform here: [link]

If you tell me your preferred distance, I can point you to a better option.
```

When someone sends `GUIDE`:

```text
Sure. Is the main issue that the trail is unknown, you do not want to ride alone, or you want someone local to plan the ride?
```

When someone sends `KORA`:

```text
Nice. Are you preparing for distance, climbing, bike check, or just building confidence before the event?
```

## First 14 Days

Post once per day if possible. If that is too much, post 4 times per week and do stories daily.

```text
DM `RIDE` and we will suggest a weekend trail direction.

This is part of what we are building with LocoXperts: local trails, local guides, and better ride planning for Nepal.
```

CTA:

> DM `RIDE`

## Comment Strategy

Spend 20 minutes per day commenting on local cycling posts.

Good comments:

- Ask about the route.
- Ask about difficulty.
- Mention safety or route condition.
- Compliment the ride without sounding promotional.

Avoid:

- Dropping links in comments.
- Generic comments like "Nice ride."
- Asking people to register before they know you.

## Weekly Metrics

Track these manually:

```text
Week:
Posts:
Stories:
Profile visits:
DMs:
Keyword DMs:
Link clicks:
Registrations:
Guide inquiries:
Ride suggestions sent:
Most common rider question:
```

Good early signal:

- 10+ DMs in a week
- 3+ riders asking for actual route help
- 1+ guide/shop conversation
- 3+ registrations from warm DMs

Bad early signal:

- Views only, no saves
- Likes only, no comments
- Link clicks but no DMs

If you get views but no DMs, make the CTA simpler and more personal:

> DM your city. I will suggest one trail idea.
